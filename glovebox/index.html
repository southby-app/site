Glovebox

MOT, tax and recalls for your vehicles.

Coming soon

Never miss an MOT or tax date.

Glovebox uses the official records for vehicles in Great Britain: MOT history and safety recalls from the DVSA, and vehicle tax from the DVLA. Add your car, van or motorbike by its registration and everything you need is in one place.

Your garage

Each vehicle with its number plate, MOT and tax at a glance, and the days left on each.

MOT and tax dates

When the MOT and tax run out, with the MOT shown as a gauge running down over its year.

Reminders

A month, two weeks and a day before the MOT or tax is due, worked out on your iPhone.

MOT history

Every MOT with its result, mileage and advisories, with the tester's notes in plainer English.

Mileage

The mileage at each MOT on a chart, with a clear flag if it ever went down.

Recalls

Any open safety recall the DVSA has for the vehicle, so you know if it needs fixing.

Your own dates

Insurance, servicing and anything else, each with its own reminders. Add any date to Calendar.

Share its history

A one-page PDF of the vehicle's MOT history, ready to share when you sell.

Look up any vehicle

Enter any registration to see its MOT, tax and recalls.

Buying Check

With Glovebox+, the things a buyer would want to know about a vehicle you look up, straight from the DVSA and DVLA. Nothing guessed or scored.

Watch out for

With Glovebox+, advisories that keep coming back MOT after MOT.

Honest information

Exactly what the DVSA and DVLA record. If something isn't in their records, Glovebox says so.

Private by design.

Glovebox has no account, no ads and no tracking. Your vehicles and dates stay on your iPhone, and only a registration is ever sent, to look it up. Read the privacy policy.

Coming soon. Glovebox is being tested now. Two vehicles are free; Glovebox+ adds as many as you like, Buying Check, Watch Out For and your choice of reminder times.
