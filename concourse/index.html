A closer look.

How it works.

  1. 1

    Pick your stations

    Nearby stations appear automatically, worked out on your iPhone. Save the ones you use and your regular journeys.

  2. 2

    Live from the rail network

    Times, platforms and disruption come from National Rail's live data, refreshed every 30 seconds or so.

  3. 3

    Tap any train for the full story

    Every stop, live expected times, how many coaches, and the reason for any delay or cancellation.

Private by design.

Concourse has no account, no ads and no tracking. Your location and favourites stay on your device. Read the privacy policy.

Concourse is an independent app and isn't affiliated with National Rail or any train operator. Always check station information before you travel.
