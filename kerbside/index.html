Kerbside

Bus times, done properly.

Coming soon

Every bus in England, in one calm app.

Kerbside is in development. It will use the Bus Open Data Service, the government's open data for every local bus in England: timetables, and where each bus actually is.

⌖

Stops near you

The stops around you and what's due at each, worked out on your iPhone.

●

Where your bus really is

Its last reported position on the map, from the bus itself. Never a guessed countdown.

◷

Timetables

Every route's timetable, by stop, for today and the days ahead.

☆

Your stops and routes

Save the stops and journeys you use, ready when you open the app.

⌕

Search

Find a stop, a route number or a place.

◌

Honest times

Planned times from the timetable, live positions as reported. If something isn't in the data, Kerbside says so.

Private by design.

Kerbside will have no account, no ads and no tracking. Your location and saved stops stay on your device. Read the privacy policy.

Coming soon. Kerbside is the next southby.app app. Email support@southby.app if you'd like to hear when it's ready to try.
