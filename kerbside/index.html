Kerbside

Bus times, done properly.

Coming soon

Know where your bus really is.

Kerbside uses the Bus Open Data Service, the government's open data for local buses in England: timetables, and where each bus actually is. Testing has started in Swindon; the rest of England follows.

Stops near you

The stops around you, nearest first, each with its buses for the next three hours.

Where your bus really is

Its last reported position on the map, and how many stops away it is. Never a guessed countdown.

Favourite stops

Star the stops you use and see their next buses as soon as you open the app.

Every line

Each line's buses running now, and its stops in each direction.

Live map

Every bus reporting around you, with stops when you zoom in. Buses silent for 15 minutes are left off.

Search

Find a stop by its name or street, or a line by its number.

Honest times

Scheduled times from the timetable, live positions as reported. If a bus isn't reporting, Kerbside says so.
