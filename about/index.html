About southby.app

Small apps, made properly.

southby.app is a small, independent app studio in Swindon, run by me, Michael. I make Apple-native apps for everyday life in Britain: rivers, trains, railtours and the telly. Every one follows the same six principles.

  1. Apple-native

    Built with Apple's own tools and design language, so each app feels familiar from the first tap. Widgets, dark mode, Dynamic Type and VoiceOver aren't extras; they're part of the job.

  2. Private by design

    No accounts, no adverts, no tracking, no analytics. If an app needs your location, it's used on your device and never sent to me. The privacy policy is short because there's very little to say.

  3. Open data, honestly credited

    Afon, Concourse and Lineside turn public UK data into something genuinely easy to use. They always say where the data comes from, and never pretend to be the official source.

  4. Small and focused

    Each release does a few things properly rather than many things badly. New features arrive when they're ready, and each app's page shows what's coming next.

  5. Fair pricing

    No adverts, ever. If an app has paid extras, they'll be optional and clearly priced, and the app will still be useful without them.

  6. A real person behind it

    Email support@southby.app and you'll hear back from the person who built the app, usually within a couple of working days.

Who's behind it

I've spent my career in IT and make apps in my own time, starting with things I wanted to use myself: Afon for river levels and flood warnings, Concourse for live train times, Settee for controlling Kodi from the sofa, and Lineside for railtours and railway specials.
