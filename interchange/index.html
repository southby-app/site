Interchange

London, live, with step-free first.

Coming soon

Get around London, step-free if you need it.

Interchange uses Transport for London's own open data: live trains and buses, line status in TfL's words, and every lift and platform as TfL records them. It's Concourse's companion for London.

Nearby

The station nearest you with its next trains, then the stations and bus stops around you.

Live trains

TfL's own predictions at every station, by line and direction.

Step-free first

Lifts, and how you board at each platform: level access, ramps, and the step and gap where TfL has measured them. Turn on Step-free First to see them before the trains.

Lifts out

Every lift out of service across the network, in TfL's own words.

Buses instead

When a lift is out, the bus stops near the station and their next buses, as a step-free way on.

Every line

Each line's status in TfL's own words, and its stops, with lifts that are out marked.

London buses

Every bus stop, with its next buses, and where each bus goes next.

Alerts

When one of your lines starts or stops having problems, and when a lift at one of your saved stations goes out or comes back.

Search

Find a station, a line, a bus stop, or a place and the stations nearest it.

Works with Concourse

At stations with National Rail trains, open Concourse for the departures.

Honest information

Shown exactly as TfL publishes it. Where TfL hasn't recorded something, Interchange doesn't guess.

Made for getting around London.

  • Wheelchair users
  • Parents with buggies
  • Anyone with heavy luggage
  • Commuters
  • Visitors to London
  • Concourse users heading into London

Private by design.

Interchange will have no account, no ads and no tracking. The stations and stops near you are worked out on your iPhone, so your location never leaves it. Read the privacy policy.
