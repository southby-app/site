How it works.

  1. 1

    Save the places you care about

    Home, the allotment, the fishing spot, the bridge you cycle over. Or just let Afon find what's near you.

  2. 2

    Afon checks the official data

    River levels and flood warnings come straight from the Environment Agency, which updates most stations every 15 minutes.

  3. 3

    You get the answer, not the numbers

    Is it low, normal, high or rising? Afon says so in plain English, with the readings there if you want them.
