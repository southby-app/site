A closer look.

How it works.

  1. 1

    Save the places you care about

    Home, the allotment, the fishing spot, the bridge you cycle over. Or just let Afon find what's near you.

  2. 2

    Afon checks the official data

    River levels and flood warnings come straight from the Environment Agency, which updates most stations every 15 minutes.

  3. 3

    You get the answer, not the numbers

    Is it low, normal, high or rising? Afon says so in plain English, with the readings there if you want them.

Private by design.

Afon has no account, no ads and no tracking. Your location and saved places stay on your device. Flood warning notifications only need the codes of the warning areas near your places, never where you are. Read the privacy policy.

Afon is not an official warning service. Always follow official advice. Check GOV.UK Check for flooding or call Floodline on 0345 988 1188. If life is at risk, call 999.
