Afon

Where does Afon's data come from?

River levels and flood warnings come from the Environment Agency's public real-time flood-monitoring service and, in Wales, from Natural Resources Wales. Afon contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0, including Natural Resources Wales information © Natural Resources Wales and Database Right. Rain forecasts come from Apple Weather. With Afon+, storm overflow data comes from Anglian Water, Northumbrian Water, Severn Trent Water, South West Water, Southern Water, Thames Water, United Utilities, Wessex Water and Yorkshire Water, each licensed under CC BY 4.0; Afon simplifies the statuses and tidies river names. Bathing water quality comes from the Environment Agency.

Is Afon free?

Yes. Flood warnings, river levels, the map, charts, rain forecasts and two saved rivers are free, and always will be. Afon+, coming soon, is a monthly or yearly subscription for people who want more: alerts at a level you choose, unlimited saved rivers, longer history and widgets. It's shared with your family, and you can cancel at any time.

Is Afon an official flood warning service?

No. Afon presents official data, but it can be delayed or unavailable. Always follow official advice. Check GOV.UK Check for flooding or Natural Resources Wales's flood warnings, sign up to their free flood warning services, or call Floodline on 0345 988 1188. If life is at risk, call 999.

How do flood warning notifications work?

When you turn them on, Afon works out on your iPhone which Environment Agency and Natural Resources Wales flood warning areas are near your places, and sends my server only those area codes and an Apple push code for your iPhone. When the Environment Agency or Natural Resources Wales issues, changes or lifts a warning in one of those areas, you get a notification. Your location is never sent. Turn them off at any time in Afon or in iOS Settings.

Which areas does Afon cover?

England and Wales, using data from the Environment Agency and Natural Resources Wales.

Why is a reading missing or out of date?

Monitoring stations sometimes go offline for maintenance or report late. Afon shows the time of each station's last reading, marks it as delayed after 6 hours and offline after 7 days, and greys it out on the map. These are problems at the station, not with Afon, and the agency that runs it is usually already fixing them.

Why does Afon ask for my location?

So it can show the stations and warnings nearest to you. Your location is only used on your device and is never sent to me: Afon only ever asks my server about specific gauges. You can say no and choose a home place in Settings instead.

Concourse

Where does Concourse's information come from?

Live train times come from National Rail's real-time data services, the same source used by station departure boards.

Why is a train showing differently from the station board?

Information can change quickly during disruption. Concourse refreshes regularly, but if in doubt, follow the station's own boards and announcements.

Why does Concourse ask for my location?

To show your nearest stations. Your location is only used on your device and is never sent to me. You can say no and search for stations instead.

Can I buy tickets in Concourse?

No. Concourse is for live times and journeys, not tickets.

Can I see trains that have already left?

Yes. Open Earlier trains at the top of a station's board or journey to see the last hour, including when each train actually left.

Where do station facilities come from?

From National Rail's station information. Tap ⓘ on any station's board to see facilities, step-free access and help points. If something looks wrong, check with the station before you travel.

Settee

How do I connect Settee to Kodi?

In Kodi, go to Settings → Services → Control and turn on 'Allow remote control via HTTP'. Make sure your iPhone and Kodi are on the same Wi-Fi, then open Settee and it should find Kodi automatically.

Why does Settee ask for local network access?

iOS asks before any app talks to devices on your home network. Settee needs this to find and control Kodi. It doesn't talk to anything else.

Settee can't find my Kodi. What can I try?

Check that remote control via HTTP is enabled in Kodi, that both devices are on the same network, and that local network access is allowed for Settee in Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network. You can also add Kodi manually by its address.

Is Settee made by the Kodi team?

No. Settee is an independent app and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by the Kodi project or the XBMC Foundation. Kodi® is a registered trademark of the XBMC Foundation.

Lineside

Where does Lineside's information come from?

From Network Rail's open data, through the Rail Data Marketplace: the published timetable, plans added at short notice, and live train movements. It's the same information the railway runs on, but specials can still change or be cancelled at short notice.

Why can't I see which locomotive is on a train?

It isn't published in Network Rail's open data. The timetable says a train is loco-hauled, but not which locomotive, or whether it's steam or diesel. Lineside won't guess.

Why isn't a train I know about showing?

Check the train types you've chosen: test trains and freight specials are off to start with. Some specials are only planned a day or two ahead, and a few are never published in open data.

How does Lineside know which station is nearest me?

Your iPhone works it out from each train's route. Your location is never sent to me. You can say no to location and still see every train.

Where can I watch from?

Stations, public footpaths and bridges. Never go onto the railway or trespass on railway land, and follow any instructions from railway staff.
