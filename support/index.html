Afon: frequently asked questions

Where does Afon's data come from?

River levels and flood warnings come from the Environment Agency's public real-time flood-monitoring service. Afon contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0.

Is Afon an official flood warning service?

No. Afon presents official data, but it can be delayed or unavailable. Always follow official advice. Check GOV.UK Check for flooding, sign up to the Environment Agency's free flood warnings, or call Floodline on 0345 988 1188. If life is at risk, call 999.

Which areas does Afon cover?

England, using Environment Agency data. Coverage for Wales is planned.

Why is a reading missing or out of date?

Monitoring stations sometimes go offline for maintenance or report late. Afon shows the time of each reading so you can see how recent it is. If a station has stopped updating for a long time, please let me know.

Why does Afon ask for my location?

So it can show the stations and warnings nearest to you. Your location is only used on your device and is never sent to Southby. You can say no and use saved places or search instead.

Do I need an account?

No. Afon doesn't have accounts, and your saved places are stored on your device.

How do I request a refund?

App Store purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
