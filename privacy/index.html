Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

In short: My apps don't have accounts, adverts, analytics or tracking. I don't collect, sell or share your personal data. Anything an app needs, such as your location, saved places or Kodi address, stays on your device. The only personal data I receive is what you choose to send me by email.

Who I am

Southby is the trading name of Michael Southby, an individual developer based in the United Kingdom. For the purposes of UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018), I am the data controller for any personal data described in this policy.

Contact: privacy@southby.app

This policy covers

  • Afon for iPhone and iPad
  • Concourse for iPhone and iPad
  • Settee for iPhone and iPad
  • Any other app published by Southby that links to this policy
  • This website, southby.app

What all my apps have in common

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising, analytics or third-party tracking code.
  • Location, if you allow it, is used on your device only. It is never sent to me or stored by me. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services.
  • Saved items and settings are stored on your device. If you use iCloud backups, they may be included in your backup, which Apple handles under its own privacy policy. I can't access it.
  • Any notifications are generated on your device.

Afon

To show river levels and flood warnings, Afon requests data directly from the Environment Agency's public flood-monitoring service. As with any internet request, the Environment Agency's servers will see your device's IP address. The request doesn't include your location or any identifier from Afon. The Environment Agency's own privacy notice applies to its services.

Concourse

To show live train times, Concourse requests information about the stations and services you look at from National Rail's data services. These requests may pass through a small server I run, which forwards them and returns the results. The request contains only the station or service you asked about, never your location or any identifier from Concourse. Like any web server, mine sees your device's IP address. I don't link it to you or use it for anything except keeping the service secure and working, and server logs are deleted within 14 days.

Settee

Settee communicates only with Kodi on your own local network. It needs iOS's Local Network permission to find and control Kodi, and it doesn't contact any other device or service. The address of your Kodi, and any username and password you enter for it, are stored on your device, with the password kept in the iOS Keychain. Nothing about your library, what you watch or your network is sent to me.

Information from Apple

Apple may provide me with aggregated, anonymous App Store information, such as download numbers. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may also share anonymised crash reports and usage statistics. I use these only to fix problems and improve the apps. They do not identify you.

When you contact me

If you email support@southby.app or privacy@southby.app, I receive your email address, your name if you include it, and whatever you write. I use this only to reply and help with your query. My lawful basis is legitimate interests: responding to people who get in touch. I keep support emails for up to 24 months, then delete them, unless I need them longer to resolve an ongoing issue. Email is delivered through my email provider, which processes it on my behalf.

This website

southby.app uses no cookies, analytics or tracking. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security and operational purposes under its own privacy statement.

Sharing your data

I do not sell, rent or share personal data with anyone, except where required by law.

International transfers

My service providers (such as my email, website and server hosts) may process data outside the UK. Where they do, appropriate safeguards required by UK data protection law apply.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to ask for a copy of your personal data, and to ask me to correct, delete or restrict it, or to object to how I use it. Since my apps don't collect personal data, this mainly applies to emails you've sent me. To make a request, email privacy@southby.app. I'll respond within one month.

If you're unhappy with how I've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk. I'd appreciate the chance to put things right first.

Children

My apps are suitable for general audiences and do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If an app's data practices change, for example if a future feature needs a server, I'll update this policy before the change goes live and revise the date at the top of this page.
