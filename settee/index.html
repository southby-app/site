How it works.

  1. 1

    Turn on remote control in Kodi

    One switch in Kodi's settings: Services, then Control, then allow remote control over HTTP.

  2. 2

    Open Settee on the same Wi-Fi

    It finds Kodi by itself. No IP addresses, no port numbers, no fiddling.

  3. 3

    Sit back

    Browse your library, play something, and control it from your phone, the Lock Screen or Control Centre.
