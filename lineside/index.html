Know what's coming down the line.

The trains you won't find on a departure board, from Network Rail's own timetable and live running.

Today's specials

Railtours, empty coaches, light engines, test trains and freight specials running today, in time order.

Choose your trains

Railtours, empty coaches and light engines, test trains, freight specials and booked freight. All on to start; turn off what you don't need in Settings.

Live running

Once a train is on its way, see where it was last reported and how early or late it's running.

Nearest to you

For each train, the station on its route closest to you, worked out on your iPhone.

Spots

Save the stations you watch from and see everything due past them today.

Spot alerts

A notification before a train passes one of your spots, and when a late-notice special is added.

Light at your spot

Front lit, side lit or into the sun, from the direction of the track and the sun at the planned time, with Apple Weather's forecast.

Follow Live

Follow a train on your Lock Screen, with an alert when it's reported a few minutes before your spot.

Coming up

Everything planned for the next seven days, day by day.

Search

Find a train by headcode, place or operator.

Late-notice trains

Specials planned at short notice appear as soon as Network Rail publishes them.

Map of the route

Each train's route drawn along the real tracks, with a pin where it was last reported.

Honest times

Times exactly as planned and reported, never guessed. If something isn't in the data, Lineside says so.

A closer look.

How it works.

  1. 1

    Choose what you're interested in

    Every type of special to start, plus booked freight near you. Turn off what you don't need.

  2. 2

    Lineside reads Network Rail's data

    The full timetable is checked every night, with late-notice plans and live train movements as they happen.

  3. 3

    Be in the right place

    See which station on the route is nearest you, when the train's due, and whether it's running early or late.

Private by design.

Lineside has no account, no ads and no tracking. Your location and spots stay on your device, and the nearest station on each route is worked out there too. Read the privacy policy.

Stay safe and legal. Watch from stations, public footpaths and bridges, and never go onto the railway. Lineside is an independent app and isn't affiliated with Network Rail or any train or charter operator.
