A closer look.

How it works.

  1. 1

    Choose what you're interested in

    Railtours, empty coach moves and light engines to start, test trains and freight specials if you want them.

  2. 2

    Lineside reads Network Rail's data

    The full timetable is checked every night, with late-notice plans and live train movements as they happen.

  3. 3

    Be in the right place

    See which station on the route is nearest you, when the train's due, and whether it's running early or late.

Private by design.

Lineside has no account, no ads and no tracking. Your location and spots stay on your device, and the nearest station on each route is worked out there too. Read the privacy policy.

Stay safe and legal. Watch from stations, public footpaths and bridges, and never go onto the railway. Lineside is an independent app and isn't affiliated with Network Rail or any train or charter operator.
